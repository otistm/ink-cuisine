# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- First playable version. You open a restaurant on a busy street and have one season, eight Friday nights, to earn stars in the Quill Guide.
- Set it up your way: name it (or roll a name), pick a cuisine (French, Italian, Japanese, Indian, Mexican or Nordic) and a vibe (candlelit, rustic farmhouse, sleek and modern, neon diner or garden terrace). Each vibe brings different guests: how many, how much they'll pay, how patient they are and how much they care about a show on the plate.
- Hire a head chef from six, each with their own flair, skill, pace, favourite cuisine and way of talking. Then pick a sous chef and someone to run the floor.
- Plan the menu with your chef: two starters, three mains and a dessert. The chef pitches a dish; put it on, ask for something else, or push for a twist (truffle, flambéed at the table, smoked over hay…). Flair decides whether a twist lands or flops. Chefs only have so many ideas a week.
- Set every price. Guests order less of anything priced above what they think is fair, and grumble about it.
- Friday night service: seat guests from the door, take orders, serve food from the pass before it goes cold, bring the bill and clear the table. One tap does the obvious thing. Your front of house helps, slowly.
- Every guest leaves a review. Good nights build buzz, which brings bigger crowds and moves you up the city table against six rival restaurants. Anyone kept waiting too long walks out to a rival.
- The Quill Guide's inspector eats here in secret three times a season. They dine alone and take notes (so does the odd food blogger). The day after, you hear a rumour about how it went.
- Between services, the office: rework the menu, hire a line cook, a porter or a sommelier, add tables, do up the room, fit heat lamps, and check the city table.
- The books every week: takings, ingredients, wages and rent. Run out of money and the bank closes you; try that week again.
- After week 8 the Quill Guide comes out: none, one, two or three stars, alongside the rivals'.
- The season is saved at the start of each week, so "Carry on" picks up where you left off.
- Send feedback from the title, pause and end screens. Crashes are reported quietly.
- Front page with a Play button, dark mode, and it installs to the home screen like the other ink games.
